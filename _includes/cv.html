Research interests

Yan Xu works on multimodal AI at Google. He focuses on Gemini post-training for long-context video understanding and on training Gemini as a multimodal judge to guide reinforcement learning for Nano Banana and Gemini Omni.

Previously at Waymo, he worked on perception foundation models and onboard perception systems for autonomous driving. He received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Kris Kitani, where he studied in-the-wild 3D human pose estimation.

Experience & education

  1. 2025–

    Google

    Gemini · Post-Training & Evaluation

    Develops Gemini post-training for long-context video understanding using supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning fine-tuning (RLFT). Builds customer-data evaluations to assess model quality and release readiness for Gemini on Google Cloud. Trains Gemini as a multimodal judge whose scores provide RL rewards for image and video generation models, including Nano Banana and Gemini Omni.

  2. 2022–2025

    Waymo

    Software Engineer · Machine Learning

    Developed perception foundation models and a Transformer-based onboard detector for autonomous driving. Used foundation-model auto-labeling and targeted data refinement to improve long-tail pedestrian and vehicle detection. Took models from data collection and training through launch-quality evaluation and fleet deployment.

  3. 2016–2022

    Carnegie Mellon University

    Ph.D. · Advisor: Kris Kitani

    Developed methods for joint 3D human pose estimation and camera calibration in uncalibrated, wide-baseline camera networks. Combined person re-identification, tracking, and bundle adjustment for geometric estimation. Explored Transformer-based cross-camera association and learning camera pose from synthetic trajectories.
