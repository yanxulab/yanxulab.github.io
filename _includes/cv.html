Research interests

My background is in 3D perception and multi-view geometry, spanning computer vision research and autonomous driving systems.

I received my Ph.D. at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Kris Kitani, studying multi-person 3D pose estimation with uncalibrated camera networks. In December 2022, I joined Waymo to work on 3D object detection, from model development to deployment.

I’m currently exploring foundation models, post-training, and reinforcement learning through hands-on implementations and controlled experiments.
